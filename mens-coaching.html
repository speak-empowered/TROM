Take the next step in faith.

Men’s coaching with Eli starts with the season you’re in. Explore what following Jesus looks like in the decisions you face and the people you serve as a husband, father, or leader.

What you might want to work on

The Restoration Framework gives you a place to start the conversation.

Identity

Look at the roles, expectations, and old stories you’ve used to define yourself. What changes when you begin with who you are in Christ?

Healing

Make room to talk honestly about hurt and see where it still shows up in your habits and relationships.

Purpose

Consider what faithfulness looks like in this season and where to put your attention at home, at work, and in your community.

Interested in coaching?

Tell Eli a little about the season you’re in and what you’d like to work through. Ask about availability, the format, and whether the program is a fit.
