The book · Eli Brackenbury

The Restoration of Man

A journey into identity, healing, and God-anchored masculinity.

Eli draws on his own story and Scripture to help men understand the wounds and expectations that have shaped them, bring those experiences before God, and practice a different way of showing up for others.

Visit the official book page

How to work through the book

Read it with a journal nearby, or bring it to a men’s group and make room for honest discussion.

Reflect and write

Journal questions ask you to notice how past experiences and old assumptions still shape your choices.

Practice during the week

Weekly assignments put the reading to work in everyday habits, conversations, and relationships.

Make time to pray

Guided prayers give you a place to start when you’re unsure how to talk with God about what surfaced.
