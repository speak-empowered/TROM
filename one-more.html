Our mission

The One More exists to help one more person surrender fully to Jesus Christ, creating a ripple effect that transforms families, communities, and generations.

We believe every person matters to God and every life can be restored through Jesus. Our hope is to see that restoration reach beyond one person into families, churches, and communities.

    The One More Podcast is envisioned as a place for honest conversations about faith, marriage, fatherhood, leadership, and restoration.

  2. Learn

    Explore coaching with Eli and consider what daily surrender to Christ looks like in your relationships and responsibilities.

  5. Lead

    Live out your faith in the way you serve your family, church, and community.

  6. Reach

    Think of one person who could use the hope you’ve received. Make time to listen, pray for them, or share part of your story.
