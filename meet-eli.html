Meet Eli

Eli Brackenbury

Eli is a writer, father, and environmental health and safety professional. At fifty, an unexpected encounter with God changed the way he understood faith and the direction of his life.

Watch Eli’s storyVisit his author site

Finding God at fifty

By midlife, Eli had been through divorce, grief, and years of feeling distant from God. His awakening began a season of looking honestly at his own life and discovering what a relationship with Jesus could look like in ordinary moments. He tells that story in his first book, Finding God at 50.

In The Restoration of Man, Eli turns to questions he’s wrestled with himself. How do you recognize the wounds shaping your choices? What does it mean to receive your identity from God? And what changes at home when you start living from that identity?

That message is at the heart of The One More, where Eli invites others to take their next step of faith and share that hope with someone else.
